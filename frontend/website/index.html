The everyday problem

Your clinic shouldn't need another complicated system.

Patients wait at reception. Prescriptions get lost. Old reports sit in WhatsApp chats. The next visit starts from scratch.

Tap2Med keeps the useful parts connected without asking your clinic to rebuild the way it works.

How Tap2Med works

Four steps. That's it.

01

Patient scans

A QR code at reception lets the patient join the queue using their own phone.

02

Clinic sees the queue

The doctor or receptionist can see who is waiting and who is next.

03

Doctor writes

The consultation stays simple. Write the prescription and finish the visit.

04

Patient comes back

Previous visits and prescriptions are available when the patient returns.
