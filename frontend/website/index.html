The everyday problem

A busy clinic shouldn't need more software to run smoothly.

Patients wait at reception. Paper prescriptions disappear. Old reports sit in WhatsApp chats. The next visit starts from scratch.

How Tap2Med works

Four simple steps.

01

Patient scans

A QR code at the clinic lets the patient join the queue from their phone.

02

Clinic sees the queue

Reception and the doctor can see who is waiting and who is next.

03

Doctor writes

Write the prescription without changing the way you already work.

04

Next visit is easier

Previous visits and prescriptions stay available when the patient returns.
